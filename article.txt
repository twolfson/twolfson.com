# 2025-02-16
What not to do as a founder:
(pitfalls from places I haven't stuck around too long, or stuck around longer than I should've)
- No time for employees or collaboration (i.e. multi-day gap, vs same or next day)
- No ownership of product, yet fixating on sales
- Hiring to try to accelerate / gain bandwith, without matching acceleration/revenue
- Dragging out long decisions (not 0-2 days, but months)
- Raise money for the sake of raising money (grants, VC, etc) -- This all take time + needs a purpose (e.g. hiring)
- Hiring only junior engineers to save money -- Always leads to faster accrual of tech debt (though have seen acquisitions still)
- Delay and delay and delay technical debt -- Can get acquired still, but adds progressively to friction, reducing velocity + increasing risk
- Treat 1 employee differently than others (e.g. 1 gets early exercise, others don't)
- Treat employees differently than a peer (i.e. lower power structure) (e.g. not worth listening to)
- Regularly listen to feedback but never take action on any piece of it, or work to find middleground -- People do leave because of this
